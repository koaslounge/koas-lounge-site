Before you submit

Give us enough to hear the fit.

A complete submission helps us review your act faster and makes it easier to match you with the right night, audience, and production setup.

  • One strong live-performance link is required
  • Include your normal lineup and approximate set length
  • Be specific about sound, stage, and equipment needs
  • Share realistic availability and compensation expectations
  • Use one submission per artist, band, or performance project
A strong Koa's fit

Professional communication, reliable arrival times, audience awareness, and a sound that works in an intimate lounge environment all matter.

Submitting this form does not guarantee a booking, date hold, rate, or performance agreement. A booking is confirmed only after direct written communication from Koa's Lounge.

What happens next

A submission starts the conversation.

Our team reviews the information you send, considers the fit for upcoming programming, and follows up when there is a realistic opportunity to discuss.

01

We review the act

We listen, watch the performance links, and consider sound, professionalism, audience fit, and production needs.

02

We match the right night

Not every act fits every event. We look for dates and concepts where the performance can succeed.

03

We contact you directly

If there is a potential fit, the Koa's team will contact the primary booking person to discuss details.
