# Business Hours

Edit one file to change hours across the entire website:

`assets/js/business-config.js`

The footer, contact page, and Open Now / Closed indicator all read from this file.

Current schedule:
- Sunday–Monday: Closed
- Tuesday–Thursday: 5 PM–10 PM
- Friday–Saturday: 5 PM–12 AM

The live status uses the `Pacific/Honolulu` time zone and refreshes every minute.
